# Supporting Information — Independent known-truth sensitivity boundaries

**Status:** post-primary, development-only external stress tests. None of the analyses below is part of the original M5 prospective denominator or a new promotion attempt.

## Primary M5 benchmark (not recomputed)

The primary study froze seeds 74001–74020 and all six KT-A–F rules before first opening. Its observed outcomes remain:

- positive information-state recovery **71/80**;
- favorable false attribution among replaceable targets **0/700**;
- unresolved-state over-resolution **0/60**;
- structural-refusal violations **0/60**;
- sharp calls when unavailable **0/120**;
- all six promotion gates passed.

The study assessed a specified stage-P/stage-T design within its declared simulator and process-information estimand. Later, differently configured tests must not replace this denominator.

## Sensitivity study A: matched random-cell versus spatial validation

A separately frozen experiment evaluated eight simulation worlds at three new seeds per world (**24 world–seed cases**). The reference was an occurrence-distribution information-state oracle (ODO), rather than a claim about real physiological causal processes. The same simulated cases, occurrence samples and reference states were tested with two validation splits and a frozen HGB plus standardized L2 logistic agreement rule.

| Metric | Random-cell 3-fold | Spatial 3-fold |
|---|---:|---:|
| Jointly authorized cases | 18/24 | 15/24 |
| Positive ODO information recovered | 3/12 | 0/12 |
| False calls among ODO-replaceable | 0/105 | 0/105 |
| Over-resolved ODO-unresolved | 2/9 | 2/9 |
| Sharp calls when ODO-unavailable | 0/18 | 0/18 |

The two unsafe observation-confounded cases appeared in both split modes; four table cells are **not four independent failures**. HGB alone recovered 7/12 positive targets under random-cell and 8/12 under spatial validation, so zero spatial consensus recovery is not proof that no process signal exists under spatial separation. Rather, the strict linear/HGB agreement rule lost information in this configuration.

## Sensitivity study B: nonlinear learner and known-effort probe

A further independently frozen experiment opened **18 cases** (six worlds × seeds 92001–92003), comprising 108 ODO process states: 12 positive, 75 replaceable, 3 unresolved and 18 unavailable. The pre-oracle no-refit audit covered 4,536 fitted route/fold records and independently recomputed 216 full-system permutation P-values from saved out-of-fold predictions, with no uncertified full route.

The *primary representation* did not supply true sampling effort.

| Metric | Random HGB + L2 | Random HGB + RF | Spatial HGB + L2 | Spatial HGB + RF |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jointly authorized cases | 12/18 | 14/18 | 8/18 | 10/18 |
| Positive ODO information recovered | 2/12 | 3/12 | 0/12 | **4/12** |
| False favorable calls among replaceable | 0/75 | 0/75 | 0/75 | 0/75 |
| Over-resolved unresolved | 1/3 | 1/3 | 1/3 | 1/3 |
| Sharp calls when unavailable | 0/18 | 0/18 | 0/18 | 0/18 |

**Observation-confounded thermal, seed 92003** was ODO-unresolved but both learner pairs called it contributory under both split modes. Thus nonlinear consensus recovered some otherwise missed positive states, but two learners agreeing did not remove a *shared observation-process bias*.

A separately frozen secondary sensitivity supplied the simulator's true, cell-level effort as a nuisance feature in every full and knockout model. This changed the representation system and cannot be treated as a controlled like-for-like accuracy improvement against the unchanged ODO. The observation-confounded thermal states were still sharpened: **3/3** under random-cell and **1/3** under spatial validation for both pairs, relative to the unchanged unresolved reference. No scientific result in this section depends on choosing a more favorable learner after observing results.

## Boundary for the submitted M5 article

The three studies differ in seed roster, oracle reference, learner panel, stage definitions, information representations and/or validation geometry. **Their numerators, denominators and pass/fail decisions cannot be pooled or compared as a controlled common effect size.**

Taken together, they narrow interpretation rather than overturn the primary endpoint:

1. The M5 implementation passed its own prospective known-truth gates.
2. Positive-state recovery can be sensitive to learner compatibility and validation design.
3. Full-system authorization or learner consensus is not by itself a guarantee against shared observation confounding.
4. Supplying an oracle effort nuisance covariate can change the target information system without resolving the confounding question.
5. None establishes species-level causal physiology, empirical ecological transportability or universal false-positive control.

The independent real-plant applicability sequence and its negative final result are disclosed separately in the manuscript's empirical-attempts supplement. No threshold, denominator, learner or decision of the original M5 test is reopened here.

# Sealed answer-check separator v25 — consumed-development contract

## Question

Can a genuinely new outcome source shrink a v23 co-supported process set without deleting a truly active member?

v25 tests one concrete separator class: **outer spatial occurrence evidence that was sealed before v21 support construction and never used to create the v21 support calls**.

## What is independent

v21 constructs process support from the model-pool occurrence rows. The occurrence split is frozen from identities and coordinates before ecological feature use. v25 reuses the already-frozen upstream hypotheses/models, but its **separator outcome evidence** comes from the answer-check occurrence rows that were not available to v21 support construction.

The comparison background used to score that answer-check evidence is also row-disjoint from both v21 occurrences and the v21 support background. A transformation or reranking of v21 scores alone does not qualify.

This is an external-transfer separator: hypotheses may be trained upstream, but the evidence that can remove a supported member is an unopened outcome source.

## Process-exclusion contrast

For every v23-supported process `p` in one simulated family/seed:

1. fit the frozen model-spec roster on v21 model-pool occurrences and the original support background;
2. fit a matched model in which **every declared representation** in the process closure of `p` is absent;
3. freeze a deterministic prediction receipt before any answer-check occurrence is opened;
4. open the answer-check occurrences only after that receipt exists;
5. evaluate baseline and excluded models separately in each sealed spatial block against a source-disjoint reference background;
6. compute balanced presence/background density-ratio log scores.

For model specification `m` and sealed block `b`:

`delta_mb = excluded_log_score_mb - baseline_log_score_mb`.

The inherited density non-inferiority margin remains **0.01 nats**, with **one SEM**. No value is calibrated from v25 outcomes.

## Evidence states

Across the frozen model-spec roster:

- `exclude`: every required model is complete and its lower one-SEM delta bound is at least `-0.01`;
- `compatible`: at least one complete required model has an upper one-SEM delta bound below `-0.01`, i.e. process exclusion clearly harms sealed transfer;
- `indeterminate`: complete evidence exists but intervals overlap the boundary;
- `unavailable`: the required model roster or sealed coverage is incomplete.

Coverage requires at least **10 complete sealed occurrences** across at least **2 distinct sealed spatial blocks**. These values were inherited from the pre-existing future outer-answer-check contract.

Only v24 may use `exclude` to remove a member. `compatible`, `indeterminate` and `unavailable` retain it.

## Context scope

The outer separator is indexed by family × seed × process. Its result is applied to every v23 context in that family/seed in which that process is supported. This is deliberate: v23 support is context-indexed, whereas v25 asks whether the process survives a separately sealed cross-context transfer challenge.

## Consumed development endpoint

Seeds `17001–17010` and all six existing benchmark families are already consumed by v21 and may be used only for diagnosis.

The truth-blind sequence is:

1. download the authoritative v23 `context_sets.csv`;
2. generate all required sealed separator evidence;
3. classify v25 evidence states;
4. apply the frozen v24 refinement operator;
5. write the refined sets and immutable truth-blind receipt;
6. only then construct known-truth labels and score deletions.

The development advancement screen is intentionally simple and safety-first:

- removed true members must equal **0**;
- removed false members must be **at least 1**.

Passing this screen does **not** establish performance and does not authorize empirical validation. It only permits a separately frozen unused-seed prospective successor. Failure records a negative development endpoint and stops before fresh seeds are spent.

## Hard stops

- no changes to v21 support thresholds;
- no use of v22 pairwise winners;
- no process-name penalty;
- no post-outcome margin change;
- no replacement of the separator after truth is opened;
- no fresh seeds in this PR;
- no empirical controls in this PR;
- no claim that a supported/refined set is a formal confidence set or causal identified set.

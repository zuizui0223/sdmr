## New algebraic identification boundary (separate from all known-truth denominators)

An exact two-world binary construction now proves that *the full observable
distribution* can coincide even when one population has a genuine X–Y
response contrast of **1/2** and the other has **0**: outcome-dependent
recording creates the same observations. Even the marginal inclusion
probability `P(S=1|X)=1/2` is identical in both worlds. This is an
**analytic nonidentifiability result**, not a new forest species result or
a second prospective KT experiment. It explains why source metadata
adequacy or learner consensus must not be called proof of ignorability.
See [the exact proof and no-data regression tests](SDMR_STRUCTURAL_SELECTION_NONIDENTIFIABILITY_V1.md).

## One scientific theme, with two independent endpoints

1. **Process-information methodology (SDMR main / M5 / Structural KT):** A predictor may be correlated with the observed response even when its information is fully carried by other variables, when sampling effort makes the signal unsafe, or when the response operator changes. Ask whether an *entire declared process carrier set* is removable while the response remains independently predictable and its observation process is sufficiently audited.
2. **Forest structural ecology (exploratory PR #284 and Structural PR #469):** Spatially predictive stand/context information does not establish that within-site temporal change or a manipulated biotic interaction causes the observed demographic or community outcome. Forest endpoints remain heterogeneous (live-to-live growth versus detected beetle guild composition versus same-site health), and are not a single meta-analytic effect size.

These are a coherent motivation for a methods paper. They are **not** yet a demonstrated cross-taxon ecological law.

## Immutable boundaries

- Original Product-A v2.8.4: `empirical_confirmation_not_supported`, decision `not_promoted`. Product B remains formally **blocked**.
- Original M5 synthetic 71/80 and 0/700 are distinct from Structural 30/30 and 0/50; never add denominators or reinterpret the original 80 M5 cases.
- Fresh real-v5: only 10/50 primary evaluable taxa and 16/300 stable sharp cells; the original endpoint failed before answer-check. Do not classify the other 40 as biological nulls.
- PR #284 and #286 remain intentionally **draft**. Previously inspected field outcomes cannot be relabeled as independent prospective confirmation.
- Original-source bytes/hashes, source opportunity, effort and risk-set identity cannot be inferred from a derived analysis artifact ZIP's SHA-256.
- Confidence intervals obtained by postfit county resampling do not automatically cover model-selection, source-selection or cohort-refitting uncertainty.

## Highest-value next independently falsifiable check

**Original-source provenance → physical sampling opportunity → prospective field endpoint**, in that order. For a new forest source, freeze the original publisher version and a verified raw-file digest (do not confuse it with a derived artifact), original physical site/plot/tree identity, actual effort and zero/not-surveyed state, attrition/risk set and taxon operator. Then predeclare independent outer blocks, full process-carrier registry, reference model and minimal ecologically meaningful heldout gain before opening any newly chosen biological response.

The source-only program currently has **no field cohort eligible for a new full SDMR process-identity confirmation**. A fail-closed source admission is a useful method result, not ecological evidence of zero effect.

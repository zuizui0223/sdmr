## Why Nature Ecology & Evolution is now a justified challenge

The paper is no longer only a conceptual decomposition of prediction, necessity and stability. It contains a prospectively developed and independently replicated estimator for **environmental-process membership under controlled truth**.

The evidence sequence is important:

1. prediction/stable surfaces can miss process truth;
2. ecological Pareto sharpening can create false necessity;
3. exclusion-based necessity controls false-required claims but can remain broad;
4. the v2.7.2 consensus-first stable core supplied a useful proof of concept but did not independently vary all principal process identities;
5. a stronger factorial test varied temperature, water and soil over all seven non-empty process combinations and falsified that predecessor as a general process classifier (**22/35 exact**, AUC **25/35**);
6. Product A then defined a process-specific counterfactual estimator based on the ecological recovery lost when all declared representations of a process are excluded from the prediction-adequate candidate class;
7. fresh validation on unused seeds 4301–4305 recovered **30/35 (85.7%)** complete process sets and passed every frozen support gate;
8. unchanged independent replication on unused seeds 4401–4410 recovered **65/70 (92.9%)** complete process sets, versus **56/70 (80.0%)** for AUC and **49/70 (70.0%)** for the predecessor stable core;
9. fresh empirical v2.8.4 remains an external-validity boundary rather than a rescued success: strict ecological improvement was 0/3 and ecological/AUC roles selected identical candidates/predictors in 108/108 matched cells.

## Principal controlled-truth result

Independent 70-case replication of the unchanged counterfactual estimator:

- exact complete process-set recovery: **65/70 = 92.9%**;
- exact recovery when the two ecological fitted models disagreed: **27/30 = 90.0%**;
- temperature sensitivity/specificity: **1.000 / 0.933**;
- water sensitivity/specificity: **1.000 / 0.933**;
- soil sensitivity/specificity: **0.975 / 1.000**;
- paired exact sets versus AUC: counterfactual-only 14, AUC-only 5, both exact 51, both wrong 0.

The descriptive AUC comparison is useful but is not converted post hoc into a universal-superiority hypothesis test.

## What is actually new

Do not claim novelty for prediction versus explanation, functional-accuracy limitations, spatial CV/tuning, variable-importance instability, Rashomon sets or sampling-bias correction.

The stronger contribution is:

> **After a simpler process-stability estimator failed a prospective factorial truth test, process membership was reformulated as a counterfactual ecological-recovery question and then recovered at 85.7% exact-set accuracy in fresh validation and 92.9% in an unchanged independent replication across all seven non-empty combinations of three independently varying environmental processes.**

This turns the paper from a warning about ecological interpretation into a concrete ecological-inference method with a falsified predecessor, fresh validation and independent replication.

## Nature editorial risk

NEE remains a high-risk target because the empirical plant corpus has no literal generating-process answer key and ecological/AUC selection collapsed to the same fitted solution in 108/108 matched cells. Thus the paper still lacks a real-data case in which counterfactual process identification can be externally verified against true biological process membership.

However, the earlier concern that the paper only raised an identification problem is substantially reduced: the controlled-truth problem is now directly solved and independently replicated.

## What can and cannot be claimed

It supports advance planning of tree identity, census opportunity, detection/attrition and proper spatial-block source audit with a prospective 2013→2018 and 2018→2023 field operator *if later authorized*.

One SCBI forest × four time periods is **one forest**, not four replicated sites; stem files are not automatically independent trees. The 2018 crown sample came from selected cored/dendroband trees and cannot be treated as full-cohort prior canopy exposure.

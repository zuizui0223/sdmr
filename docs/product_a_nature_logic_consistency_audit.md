# Nature logic consistency audit — Product A

Status: **reporting-only logic audit after final counterfactual validation/replication**.

## Audit question

Does the Nature-track manuscript consistently distinguish process membership, process necessity, predecessor process stability and empirical non-identification after the new factorial/counterfactual evidence?

## Finding

The final manuscript must distinguish **three** controlled-truth process-level objects rather than treating them as one estimator.

### Estimand A — counterfactual process membership (final positive estimator)

Question:

> How much best attainable held-out ecological niche recovery is lost when every declared representation of process `p` is excluded from the prediction-adequate candidate class?

Estimator:

- prediction adequacy is imposed first;
- candidate predictors are mapped to the frozen process registry, including aliases such as `temp_proxy → temperature`;
- process-containing and fully process-excluded adequate model classes are compared on best held-out Schoener-D niche overlap within each of five predeclared perturbations;
- normalized overlap loss is averaged across perturbations;
- process-specific thresholds were calibrated only on discovery truth and frozen before validation.

Prospective evidence:

1. factorial discovery/falsification (`4201`–`4205`, n=35): predecessor stable core only **22/35** exact;
2. fresh validation (`4301`–`4305`, n=35): counterfactual **30/35** exact, all T/W/S sensitivity/specificity gates pass;
3. unchanged independent replication (`4401`–`4410`, n=70): counterfactual **65/70** exact; T sens/spec1.000/0.933, W1.000/0.933, S0.975/1.000; exact process truth **27/30** when ecological fitted models disagree.

Interpretation:

> Counterfactual ecological-recovery loss identifies **process membership under the declared representation registry** in controlled truth.

It is not physiological causation or fundamental-niche necessity.

### Estimand B — exclusion-based process necessity

Question:

> Does any adequate ecological explanation survive when declared process information is excluded under the stronger necessity evidence contract?

v2.6 result:

- false-required=0;
- possible-process recall=1.0;
- possible-process precision≈0.467;
- `required_processes` empty in 9/9 validation taxa.

Interpretation:

> Exclusion-based necessity controls false-required claims but can remain broad and unresolved.

It is distinct from the final process-membership estimator. **Do not call 65/70 a necessity-recovery rate.**

### Estimand C — consensus-first process stability (predecessor evidence)

Question:

> Do canonical ecological and perturbation-robust selectors support the same process information despite fitted-model differences?

Earlier v2.7.2 stable core:

- exact hidden process sets 55/60 in the original suite;
- process-set consensus50/60 versus exact-model consensus38/60;
- exact process set19/22 when fitted models disagreed;
- pooled precision0.9889, recall/F10.9833.

But temperature and water were true in every one of those 60 cases. The stronger factorial process truth subsequently reduced this predecessor to **22/35** exact. Therefore v2.7.2 is a proof-of-concept/mechanism result, not the final presence/absence performance claim.

## Correct integrated logic

The manuscript sequence must be:

1. prediction and stable surfaces can be ecologically misattributed;
2. ecological model-set pruning can create false necessity;
3. the stable-process predecessor can retain useful process information but fails a stronger independently varying process-membership test (22/35);
4. counterfactual ecological-recovery loss is developed from that falsification;
5. thresholds are frozen on discovery evidence only;
6. fresh validation succeeds at30/35;
7. the unchanged estimator independently replicates at65/70 with high T/W/S sensitivity/specificity;
8. the stronger necessity certificate remains a separate broad safety estimator;
9. fresh empirical plant data remain non-identifying and non-promoted (108/108 selector collapse; strict improvement0/3).

## Forbidden conflations

Do not state or imply:

- 65/70 measures physiological, causal or fundamental-niche necessity;
- the counterfactual estimator is identical to v2.6 process knockouts;
- the counterfactual estimator is identical to v2.7.2 `stable_process_core`;
- 55/60 is the final validated presence/absence process-identification rate;
- discovery 4201–4205 cases are part of validation or replication;
- 65/70 versus56/70 proves universal superiority over AUC;
- v2.8.4 became empirically supported because controlled-truth performance improved;
- the current declared process registry closes every possible real-world proxy/composite route.

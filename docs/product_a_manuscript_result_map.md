## Final compression rule

1. **Prediction/stability can miss process truth.**
2. **Agreement among selected good models can create false necessity.**
3. **Explicit exclusion can control false necessity but may remain broad.**
4. **A consensus-first predecessor looked strong but failed once temperature, water and soil were independently toggled: 22/35 exact.**
5. **Counterfactual process-specific niche-recovery loss passed fresh validation at 30/35 and unchanged independent replication at 65/70.**
6. **Fresh empirical plant data remain bounded: strict advantage not supported and selectors identical in 108/108 cells.**

## Estimator separation

Do not compare v2.6 possible-process precision≈0.467, v2.7.2 stable-core P=0.9889 and final 65/70 counterfactual exact-set recovery as though they are successive values of one metric. They answer different questions:

- v2.6: necessity/possibility under exclusion;
- v2.7.2: stability across ecological selectors;
- final successor: process membership inferred from counterfactual ecological-recovery loss.

Workflow/artifact identities belong in Methods/provenance, except the independent replication provenance should remain visible because it establishes unchanged-method replication.

# Product-A v1→v2.8.4 manuscript synthesis

Status: **authoritative manuscript synthesis / no new scientific authorization**.

The empirical endpoint remains v2.8.4 `empirical_confirmation_not_supported`, followed by `not_promoted`. Known-truth support is preserved. Product B remains blocked.

## 1. Manuscript thesis

Product A does not establish a universally superior SDM tuner. It establishes that occurrence-only ecological interpretation contains several inferential objects that winner-based model selection can collapse incorrectly:

1. predictive/functional adequacy;
2. process-information necessity;
3. process-information stability across ecological selectors;
4. substitutable/contested/unresolved alternatives;
5. evidence availability and computational identity.

Central claim:

> **Predictive success does not identify ecological necessity. Necessity should be challenged by excluding declared process information and asking whether adequate alternatives survive, while process stability should be evaluated separately across defensible ecological selectors.**

Neither process-level estimand is a complete causal or fundamental-niche claim.

## 2. How the target changed prospectively

The original Product-A target was one full transferable protocol behind sealed spatial and unseen-taxon barriers. Known-truth development then falsified increasingly strong interpretations:

- v2.1–v2.2: prediction/environmental recovery and stable surfaces did not guarantee generating-process attribution;
- v2.3: ecological Pareto sharpening could narrow a certificate while losing truth coverage and creating false necessity.

The project therefore moved from “which protocol wins?” to “what ecological claims are identified, stable, substitutable or unresolved?” without changing the consumed empirical endpoint after seeing its result.

## 3. Evidence sequence

### Result 1 — Prediction/stability ≠ process truth

**Evidence:** v1, v2.1, v2.2.

Prediction metrics remain useful adequacy checks, but predictive transfer or stable response surfaces do not by themselves justify process claims.

### Result 2 — Agreement among ecologically good models ≠ necessity

**Evidence:** v2.3.

Pareto certificates were sharper in all three controlled-truth panels but failed truth-coverage criteria and could create false necessary-process claims. Intersections within a performance-filtered set are conditional on that filtering rule.

### Result 3 — Exclusion-based necessity can control false-required claims, with broad/unresolved outcomes preserved

**Evidence:** v2.4–v2.6.

Necessity was reformulated as a process-information knockout question. Missing calibration remained `unavailable` rather than being converted into absence. After prospective calibration redundancy, v2.6 produced:

- complete process certificates: 9/9;
- complete boundary certificates: 9/9;
- false-required processes: 0;
- possible-process recall: 1.0;
- possible-process precision: ≈0.467;
- wider calibrated intervals than complete-adequate intervals.

**Interpretation:** the exclusion branch controls false necessity under the declared known-truth contract, but the safe identified set can remain broad. This is not the same estimator as the v2.7.2 stable process core.

### Result 4 — Consensus-first process stability can exceed exact-model stability

**Evidence:** v2.7.1–v2.7.2.

v2.7.1 showed that process-dependent numerical behavior could alter one discrete selected-predictor result, making deterministic execution scientifically material.

v2.7.2 prospectively fixed RNG/estimator identity and evaluated 60 unused known-truth cases from six niche families in two independent processes. Its `stable_process_core` was defined as the intersection of process sets supported by the canonical ecological-recovery selector and the perturbation-robust ecological-recovery selector. It was **not** a process-exclusion necessity set.

Frozen results:

- stable-core precision = 0.9889;
- stable-core recall/F1 = 0.9833;
- process-set consensus = 50/60;
- exact fitted-model consensus = 38/60;
- observation correction = 10/10 in the confounded family and 0/50 elsewhere;
- maximum audited independent-process difference = 0.0.

**Interpretation:** process information can be highly stable and aligned with generating truth even when exact model identity is not unique. Do not attribute these P/R values to falsification-first necessity.

### Result 5 — Fresh empirical evidence supplies an observational-equivalence boundary

**Evidence:** v2.7.3–v2.8.4, with v2.8.4 as the final fresh scientific endpoint.

Structural and technical states were kept separate from ecological outcomes. v2.8.4 completed the full frozen denominator:

- prediction guardrail passed;
- ecological nondomination 3/3;
- strict ecological improvement 0/3;
- mean presence-rank delta versus AUC = 0.0;
- `empirical_confirmation_not_supported`;
- `not_promoted`.

Reporting-only audit then showed ecological and AUC roles selected identical candidates and selected predictors in 108/108 matched taxon×M×seed cells.

**Interpretation:** empirical superiority was tested and not supported. The corpus also failed to instantiate a selector-disagreement contrast, so it cannot directly validate real-data process truth or the performance of ecological identification when selection rules genuinely diverge.

## 4. Correct integrated conceptual model

### Prediction

Does the fitted model adequately rank/transfer withheld observations?

### Exclusion-based necessity

Does an adequate ecological explanation remain after declared information for process `p` is removed?

- surviving witness → necessity refuted;
- no adequate witness under a complete frozen contract → required by that contract;
- insufficient evidence → unresolved/unavailable.

### Consensus-first process stability

Do independent ecological selection principles support the same process information?

This can be strong even when exact model identity differs. It does not prove necessity.

### Representation boundary

Raster, proxy, derived exposure, composite and process are different objects. Current exclusion is relative to a declared registry; complete real-world proxy closure is a future prospective estimand.

## 5. Genuine novelty

Do not claim novelty for prediction≠explanation, spatial CV, tuning, collinearity, sampling-bias correction, variable-importance instability, Rashomon sets or partial identification themselves.

The strongest defensible novelty is their ecological-identification synthesis plus prospective evidence that:

1. ecological-recovery filtering can itself manufacture false necessity;
2. process necessity can be made falsifiable through declared process-information exclusion;
3. unresolved/unavailable evidence is retained rather than silently converted to absence;
4. process-information stability is a separate estimand and can exceed exact-model stability;
5. observation bias can affect both predictions and the withheld occurrence target;
6. deterministic computational identity can be part of a discrete scientific estimator;
7. scientific non-support, unavailability, technical failure and governance non-promotion remain distinct;
8. the unfavorable empirical endpoint is preserved without retuning.

## 6. Five main Results

1. Prediction and stable surfaces did not identify process truth.
2. Model-set sharpening could create false necessity.
3. Falsification-first exclusion controlled false necessity but remained broad.
4. Process information remained stable without unique model identity.
5. Fresh occurrence data revealed observational equivalence.

## 7. Conclusion

> **Product A changes the inferential object rather than promoting a universal winner. Ecological interpretation from occurrence-only SDMs should distinguish what predicts well, what process information is required under a declared evidence system, what process information is stable across defensible ecological analyses, and what remains unresolved. Controlled truth supports both false-necessity control and high process-information stability as separate results; the fresh empirical endpoint does not support strict advantage over AUC and instead reveals selector collapse.**

## Hard stop

No v2.9, no empirical rescue, no denominator/taxon/M/threshold/candidate change, no retroactive proxy closure and no Product-B unblock.

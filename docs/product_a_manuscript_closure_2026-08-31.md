## Manuscript claim

The paper should no longer be framed primarily as a single fresh empirical comparison that failed to improve on AUC. The full prospectively frozen v1→v2.8.4 sequence supports a broader but still bounded methodological conclusion:

> **Occurrence-only SDM tuning becomes an ecological identification problem when multiple models or environmental representations are observationally substitutable. Prediction adequacy is necessary but does not establish ecological necessity; defensible niche-driver inference requires protected information boundaries, falsification-first process tests, set-valued uncertainty, explicit unresolved/unavailable states, observation-process separation and reproducible scientific execution.**

The final fresh empirical result is the external-validity boundary on this claim, not a result to hide or repair. Under the frozen 12-taxon × three-seed × three-M endpoint, the ecological procedure passed the prediction guardrail and process-status reproducibility but did not show strict independent ecological improvement relative to the AUC-selected role.

This manuscript position does **not** establish that AUC is universally optimal, that correlation/VIF filtering is generally preferable, that environmental process domains are biologically unnecessary, or that fundamental niches, demographic fitness, dispersal history or biotic interactions were recovered.

## Scientific Results spine

The main paper should compress the implementation history into five scientific results. Version numbers belong mainly in provenance and supplementary material.

### Result 1 — Prediction transfer and stable response surfaces do not guarantee process recovery

Use Product-A v1 as the information-barrier foundation and v2.1–v2.2 as the prospective falsification of the single-winner assumption.

Retained contribution:

- whole-spatial-block model-pool versus sealed answer-check separation;
- unseen-taxon procedure-transfer barrier;
- M/background treated as sensitivity rather than score-optimized tuning;
- prediction metrics retained as adequacy checks;
- known-truth evidence showing that held-out environmental recovery or stable response surfaces can coexist with incorrect process attribution.

Methodological consequence: **prediction/model selection and ecological process identification are distinct estimands**.

### Result 2 — Agreement among retained fitted models is not biological necessity

Use v2.3 as the anti-conservative counterexample.

The ecological Pareto certificate was sharper than the complete-adequate set in all three known-truth panels but failed truth coverage and could create a false necessary-process core.

Methodological consequence: the intersection of retained fitted process sets is not automatically biological necessity, and between-model min–max spread is not automatically a complete uncertainty interval.

### Result 3 — Falsification-first set inference protects truth by allowing unresolved states

Treat v2.4–v2.6 as one scientific development family.

- v2.4: all nine validation taxa had complete process certificates, false-required processes were zero and possible-process recall was 1.0, but the full process-plus-boundary certificate was unavailable because only 18/21 required response intervals per panel were discovery-calibrated.
- v2.5: the frozen minimum calibration-support rule was retained and fresh validation was not opened when required soil boundary keys lacked enough complete calibration taxa.
- v2.6: after prospective calibration redundancy, all process and boundary certificates were complete, false-required processes remained zero, and possible-process recall was 1.0.

The v2.6 limitation must remain explicit: possible-process precision was broad at about 0.467 and calibrated intervals were wider than the complete-adequate intervals.

Methodological consequence: **conservative set width and `unavailable/unresolved` are valid scientific outputs when a narrower claim would exclude truth**.

### Result 4 — Set-valued process inference can become both safe and sharp, but deterministic execution is part of the method

Use v2.7.1 as the implementation falsification and v2.7.2 as the strongest positive Product-A result.

v2.7.1 showed that independent process/shard execution could change a discrete selected-predictor result even with fixed data, folds, candidate library and scientific rules. The deterministic successor therefore fixed estimator and selection-process RNG identity prospectively.

On 60 unused known-truth cases from six niche families in two independent process replicates, v2.7.2 produced exact output identity (observed maximum absolute and relative differences 0.0) and passed all scientific non-regression gates:

- robust ecological selector coverage = 1.000 (60/60);
- stable-process-core precision = 0.9889;
- stable-process-core recall = 0.9833;
- stable-process-core F1 = 0.9833;
- observation-confounded correction activation = 1.000 in the confounded family and 0.000 in all others.

Methodological consequence: the safety–sharpness trade-off seen in v2.6 was not permanent, and reproducible process-level identification can be both conservative in logic and sharp under informative controlled truth.

### Result 5 — Fresh empirical evidence bounds rather than erases the controlled-truth result

Use v2.7.3–v2.8.3 only to establish the distinction between structural/technical states and ecological outcomes. Use v2.8.4 as the only fresh empirical scientific endpoint.

The v2.8.4 runtime-only successor preserved the inherited scientific semantics and completed the full preregistered denominator. All three seed parts were structurally auditable and scientifically available; each contained all 12 taxa and all three 150/300/500 km M specifications. The prediction guardrail passed. The ecological procedure was nondominated relative to the AUC-selected role in 3/3 parts, and process-status reproducibility passed with minimum modal fraction 1.0.

However, strict ecological improvement occurred in 0/3 parts. The ecological and AUC roles had identical sealed metric summaries within each part and the mean presence-rank delta versus AUC was 0.0. The frozen terminal decision was `empirical_confirmation_not_supported`, followed by the separate `not_promoted` decision.

Methodological consequence: **known-truth recovery/safety and general fresh empirical superiority are separate claims, and the current evidence supports only the former**.

The final manuscript should present the v1→v2.8.4 sequence as **methodological discovery through prospective falsification**:

→ `prediction/stability ≠ process recovery`

→ `falsification + abstention`

Do not describe this as “a failed AUC competitor that changed its question.” The original niche-recovery objective was progressively refined because controlled-truth experiments falsified simpler interpretations while the frozen empirical endpoint was retained without rescue.

## Novelty position

Do not claim a new universal SDM score. The strongest novelty is the integration of:

1. sealed-before-tuning spatial and unseen-taxon information barriers;
2. explicit separation of prediction adequacy from ecological process recovery;
3. set-valued necessary / possible-substitutable / contested / unresolved process inference;
4. falsification-first necessity logic and explicit abstention when evidence products are incomplete;
5. observation-process treatment of both prediction surfaces and the held-out occurrence target;
6. deterministic execution as part of the scientific estimator when numerical variation can change discrete selected predictors;
7. prospective separation of scientific non-support, unavailable evidence and technical failure;
8. a full-denominator empirical endpoint retained unchanged when the primary ecological-support rule failed.

Individual ingredients have precedents. The claim is their ecological-identification synthesis and the prospective evidence sequence demonstrating why each separation is needed.

## Representation hierarchy and future boundary

The current Chapter-1 hierarchy may be used for interpretation but not to rescue Product A:

`geophysical template → direct environmental field → integrated biological exposure → composite summary representation`.

Raw raster, ecological process and substitutable representation are different objects. Predictor roles should therefore remain explicit: spatial geometry, substrate, direct environment, derived exposure, proxy and composite summary. A selected raster is not automatically a causal driver.

A future hierarchy-aware successor could test process-information indispensability under a predeclared representation/proxy closure. That requires a genuinely new prospective contract and independent evidence and must not be presented as evidence already supplied by v2.8.4.

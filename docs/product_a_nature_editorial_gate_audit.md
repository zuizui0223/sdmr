## Gate 1 — Ecological advance rather than software benchmark

**PASS if framed as ecological identification.**

Nature-level advance:

> predictive validation, process necessity and process-information stability are distinct inferential tasks. Necessity must be challenged against adequate alternatives; stability must be evaluated separately across defensible ecological analyses.

Prospective evidence:

- prediction/stable surfaces can miss process truth;
- ecological Pareto sharpening can create false necessity;
- exclusion-based necessity can eliminate false-required claims while preserving broad/unresolved alternatives;
- a separate consensus-first certificate shows process information can be more stable than exact model identity (`50/60` vs `38/60`);
- fresh empirical ecological/AUC selection collapses to the same fitted solution in `108/108` matched cells.

The novelty noun is **ecological identification**, not SDMR tuning performance.

## Gate 2 — Conclusions supported by evidence

**PASS after estimator separation.**

Supported:

- good-model agreement can be anti-conservative for necessity;
- v2.6 exclusion-based known-truth certificates achieved false-required=0 and possible-process recall=1.0, with a broad possible-process set (precision≈0.467);
- v2.7.2 **consensus-first** stable process cores achieved P=0.9889 and R/F1=0.9833 across 60 unused cases, with process-set consensus exceeding exact-model consensus;
- unresolved/unavailable evidence is preserved rather than converted to ecological absence;
- computational identity matters when numerical drift can alter discrete selection;
- fresh empirical strict superiority over AUC was not supported and selector contrast was absent.

Not supported/prohibited:

- v2.7.2 P/R as precision/recall of falsification-first necessity;
- stable process core as the necessary-process set;
- universal empirical superiority over AUC;
- direct empirical recovery of generating process truth;
- fundamental-niche or causal-driver claims;
- universal plant-driver claims;
- complete real-world proxy closure.

## Gate 3 — Broad ecological relevance

**PASSABLE; main editorial risk.**

The general problem arises when observational ecology combines substitutable measurements/proxies, multiple adequate fitted explanations and biological interpretation stronger than the criterion that selected the model. SDMs are the worked test bed; other ecological domains are conceptual extensions, not empirically validated here.

## Gate 4 — Empirical biological consequence

**LIMITATION.**

Fresh data did not generate divergent ecological and AUC models; they selected the same candidate/predictors in 108/108 cells. The scientifically defensible consequence is therefore an **observational-equivalence limit**, not a changed empirical biological conclusion.

Do not seek a favorable new panel to create divergence.

## Interpretation boundary: what is earned and what is not

**Methodological evidence earned:** Under eight explicitly defined data-generating worlds and source-audit metadata, a procedure separating (i) full-system adequacy, (ii) all-carrier process closure, (iii) spatial and within-site information, (iv) observation/selection eligibility and (v) endpoint operator retains positive information while avoiding positive calls in the 50 declared null/unsafe world×seed cases. The same *predeclared* rules held on unused random seeds.

**Not earned:**
1. **Unobserved selection detection:** the simulator supplies a privileged `observation_selection_known` flag. The 10/10 protective abstentions require reliable *independent* source audits; unknown selection may still fool the procedure.
2. **Causal ecology:** even same-interval time-change information can reflect site quality, harvest/survival selection, habitat history or unobserved feedback. This benchmark does not identify interventions or physiological limiting processes.
3. **Broad transport:** these 80 cases instantiate the **same eight fixed world families**, not 80 independent ecosystems, taxa or environmental response mechanisms. Repeated seeds within each world and world families sharing generator components are not biological replication.
4. **Comparative methodological dominance:** the three alternative inferential rules are deliberate ablations with identical synthetic losses; this does not establish an advantage over published hierarchical causal models, conditional permutation importance, model class reliance or every grouped-refit baseline.
5. **Empirical confirmation:** existing FIA survivor-growth signals, Finnish deadwood/Host_tree, Steigerwald six-plot beetle composition and Carpathian environment-only sources have different endpoints or evidence scopes. None was refit, opened anew or promoted by this work.
6. **SDMR Product-B generalization:** remains formally blocked by the original Product-A empirical non-promotion; this separate follow-up cannot bypass that gate.
